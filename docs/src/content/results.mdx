### Memory QA accuracy

Judged LoCoMo accuracy (%), micro-averaged over the 1,540 answerable questions:

<AccuracyTable />

Encoding each fact in isolation (*w* = 0) trails Mem0 and degrades as more facts are retrieved: from 62.2% to 53.8% on Llama and, most steeply, from 54.0% to 37.2% on Mistral as *k* grows from 5 to 50.
A context window reverses this.
With *w* of 20 or more, accuracy is flat-to-rising in *k* and comes within a few points of Mem0 (on Llama, 60.3% vs. 63.3% at *k* = 50), while often exceeding it at small *k*.
